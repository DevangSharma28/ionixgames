01

Introduction

Ionix Studio ("Ionix", "we", "us") makes games for PC, web and mobile. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you play our games, including those distributed through Google Play, the Apple App Store and the web, and when you visit this website. By playing our games or using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

02

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, address or phone number. We, and the services we use, may automatically collect:

  • Device information — device model, operating system and version, language, and advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted).
  • Usage data — sessions, levels reached, in-game events, crash reports and performance data.
  • Network data — IP address and connection type.
  • Approximate location — country or region derived from your IP address. We never collect precise GPS location.
  • Information you send us — your email address and message, if you contact us.

Third-party services built into our games may collect information on their own, under their own privacy policies (see section 04).

03

How we use it

  • To run, maintain and improve our games.
  • To understand how players use our games and balance difficulty and features.
  • To find and fix bugs and crashes.
  • To show advertising, which keeps many of our games free.
  • To process in-app purchases through the app stores.
  • To reply when you contact us, and to share important updates.
  • To meet legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse.
04

Third-party services

Depending on the game, we may use the following services. Each one handles data under its own policy:

  • Google AdMob — advertising · Privacy policy
  • Unity Ads / Unity Analytics — advertising and analytics · Privacy policy
  • GameAnalytics — gameplay analytics · Privacy policy
  • Google Play — distribution and purchases · Privacy policy
  • Apple App Store — distribution and purchases · Privacy policy
05

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With the service providers listed above, so they can deliver their services to us.
  • With app stores, to process purchases.
  • When required by law, court order or a government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety or property of Ionix, our players or others.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy still applies to your data.
06

EU / EEA users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, the EEA or the UK, we process your data based on your consent (for example, personalized ads) or our legitimate interest in running and improving our games. You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to delete your data.
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

Players in these regions see a consent prompt before any personalized advertising is shown. To exercise any right, email us using the address in section 13.

07

Children's privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13, or under 16 in the EU/EEA. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

08

Data retention

We keep analytics data for up to 24 months, then delete or anonymize it. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

09

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10

Your advertising choices

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older versions).
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

11

This website

When you visit this website (as opposed to playing our games):

  • Google Analytics — counts visits and records how the site is used: pages viewed, how far you scroll, which buttons and links you click (for example a Google Play link), your approximate country, device and browser type, and the page that referred you. It sets cookies to tell visits apart. IP addresses are not stored. · Privacy policy
  • Google Forms — receives the name, email address, subject and message you send through our contact form. · Privacy policy
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare) — deliver the site's fonts and 3D library; they receive your IP address as part of the request. · Cloudflare privacy policy
  • Your browser's local storage — keeps the site's coin counter, whether the intro animation has played, and your cookie choice. This stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland are not tracked by Google Analytics until they accept the cookie banner. Anyone can change their choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. The site shows no ads and does not use advertising cookies.

12

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Continuing to play our games after an update means you accept the revised policy.
