About

About Ionix

A small team making original games — and building the engine we wished we had along the way.

Ionix started with a simple frustration: most tools force a trade-off between speed and control. So alongside every game we ship, we've been building our own — Ion Engine — shaped entirely by what we actually needed on real projects, not a features checklist.

Day to day we're heads-down on gameplay systems, AI behaviors, camera work and UI feel, with a strong focus on scalable architecture, clean code and performance — because a game that stutters on real hardware is a game people quietly close.

  • BasedRemote-first studio
  • Founded2024
  • FocusOriginal games & engine tooling
  • TeamSmall, multidisciplinary
  • AvailabilityOpen to publishing & collaboration
Process

How We Build

The same five steps, every project, in this order.

01

Concept

Break the game down to the single mechanic that reads clearly in under three seconds.

02

Prototype

Grey-box the mechanic and get it playable same-week, before any art is final.

03

Build

Full implementation in Unity or Ion Engine, with systems built to survive scope changes.

04

Polish

Playtest relentlessly, trim what doesn't earn its place, and tune feel until it clicks.

05

Ship

Export per-platform builds, QA on real hardware, and hand off with a plan for what's next.
